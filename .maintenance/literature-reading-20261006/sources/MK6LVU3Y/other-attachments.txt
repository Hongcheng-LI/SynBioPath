                                                                Discussion


                                                                 Because the N-benzoyl transferase catalyzes the ﬁnal step in
                                                                       side chain construction, it was anticipated that this enzyme would
                                                     show a kinetic preference for the last intermediate of the Taxol bio-
                                                                     synthetic pathway N-debenzoyltaxol (6) [14]. As demonstrated in
                                                                  the present work, however, the selectivity of the N-benzoyl trans-
                                                                        ferase for N-debenzoyltaxol over N-debenzoyl-20-deoxytaxol (5) as
                                                                     substrate is quite modest (i.e., a twofold difference in kinetic efﬁ-
                                                                   ciency based on V/Km); nevertheless, it appears to be sufﬁcient,
                                                                         in combination with the speciﬁcity of the 20-hydroxylase for N-
                                                                  debenzoyl-20-deoxytaxol (5) as substrate [17], to direct the path-
                                                   way sequence as proposed by 20-hydroxylation in the side chain
                                                                 followed by N-benzoylation. The reverse sequence, involving N-
