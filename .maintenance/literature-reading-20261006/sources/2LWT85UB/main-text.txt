DISCUSSION

  Fungal secondary metabolite gene organization facilitates
the characterization of the respective pathways by stepwise dis-
section. However, a full understanding of the pathways is still
challenging, particularly when novel hypothetical proteins are
involved. The detailed knowledge of the aurofusarin pathway
was exploited to address the function of two novel classes of
proteins. This provides important new biological insight and
knowledge of the molecular machinery required for the biosyn-
thesis of a fungal pigment.
