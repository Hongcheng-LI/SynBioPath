A R T I C L E  I N F O                  A B S T R A C T

Keywords:                                         This study highlights the significance of overexpressing 1-deoxy-d-xylulose-5-phosphate synthase (DXS) from the
Bacillus subtilis                         MEP (methylerythritol 4-phosphate) pathway, in addition to short-chain prenyltransferase fusions for the
Taxadiene                                    improved production of the diterpene, taxa-4,11-diene, the first committed intermediate in the production of
Prenyltransferase                                                   anti-cancer drug paclitaxel. The results showed that the strain which has (i) the taxadiene synthase (txs) gene
                                                   integrated into the genome, (ii) the MEP pathway genes overexpressed, (iii) the fpps-crtE prenyltransferases
                                                   fusion protein and (iv) additional expression of 1-deoxy-d-xylulose-5-phosphate synthase (DXS), yielded the
                                                  highest production of taxa-4,11-diene at 390 mg/L (26 mg/L/OD600). This represents a thirteen-fold increase
                                          compared to the highest reported concentration in B. subtilis. The focus on additional overexpression of DXS and
                                                        utilizing short-chain prenyltransferase fusions underscores their pivotal role in achieving significant titer im­
                                            provements in terpene biosynthesis.

Taxadiene production is strongly boosted through additional dxs
overexpression

   To further optimize the genetic construct of taxadiene-produced
B. subtilis strains, each MEP pathway gene was overexpressed addi­
tionally based on strain txs + 8 + crtE [17] for the detection of their
influence on taxadiene production. Dxs, ispD, ispF, ispH, ispC, ispE and
ispG gene was encoded on the pBS0E plasmid separately with crtE within
the same operon under the control of xylose induced promoter (Fig. S1).
   The total amount of produced taxa-4,11-diene (mg/L) and  cell
growth of the strains with different constructs was measured and
compared  after 48 h fermentation in 2SR medium  (Fig. 3A). The
experiment was performed in triplicate. The highest taxadiene titer
came from txs + 8 + dxs_crtE strain with 313.8 mg/L (25.44 mg/L/
OD600). This confirms that the dxs gene has a strong influence on the flux
through this metabolic pathway and its expression in the 8-gene operon
was not optimal [36,37]. It is important to note that this strain has the
lowest OD of all these strains. While taxadiene production of strains with
additional expression of other genes remains similar or slightly lower
than control strain txs + 8 + crtE.
