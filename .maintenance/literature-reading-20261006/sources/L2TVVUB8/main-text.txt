  The chemical structure of aspernidine A led us to
hypothesize that it might be derived from orsellinaldehyde,
whichwasshownpreviouslytobetheproductoftheNR-PKS,
PkfA.4 To test this hypothesis, we generated a pkfA
deletion in the mpkAΔ background, replacing pkfA with
the A. fumigatus pyroA gene, a nutritional selection marker
required for pyridoxine biosynthesis. The deletion was
verified by diagnostic PCR. We cultured this mpkAΔ,
pkfAΔ double mutant and the mpkAΔ strain under the
same conditions that yielded aspernidine A. In the double
mutant,aspernidineAproductionwaseliminated(Figure1A).
This confirmed that PkfA, the PKS encoded by pkfA, is
required for the biosynthesis of aspernidine A.
