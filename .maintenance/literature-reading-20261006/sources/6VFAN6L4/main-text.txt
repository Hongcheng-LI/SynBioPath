   Thermophilic    fungi  are  commonly   found   in
self-heated   habitats,   such  as composts,   and  help
to  decompose    various   plant   materials.    How-
ever,   there   have  been  few  reports    describing
production    of  biologically    active  substances   by
these  fungi.   During  a screening  programme    for
new   antibiotics   produced   by  thermophilic    mi-
