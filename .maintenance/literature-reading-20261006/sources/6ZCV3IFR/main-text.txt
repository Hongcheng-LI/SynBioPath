        1.  Introduction

         Natural products that include a naphthopyranone (1H-naph-
          tho-[2,3-c]pyran-1-one) core structure A are widely dispersed in
          nature,  having been  isolated from  a  variety  of  sources
         including fungi, bacteria, lichen and plants. The bioactivity of
           this group, oen the directing force for isolation of these
        compounds, encompasses  antibiotic,  cytotoxic, antioxidant,
         immunoregulatory,  antimalarial  and  antifungal  activities
        amongst the diverse range of identied bioactivities. Given
          that   natural   products  and   natural   product-inspired
        compounds still represent a signicant proportion of currently
          available pharmaceuticals,1 investigation of this structure class
