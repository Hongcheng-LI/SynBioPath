Studies on Rhubarb (Rhei Rhizoma). V.l) Isolation and Characterization
of Chromone and Chromanone Derivatives
YOSHIKI KASHIWADA, GEN-ICHIRO NONAKA, and ITSUO NISHIOKA*
Faculty Of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Kyushu University 62,
3—1—1 Maidashi, Higashi-ku, Fukuoka 812, Japan
(Received February 3, 1984)
Six chromone and chromanone derivatives (I—VI) have been isolated from rhubarb
(commercial name: together with torachrysone 8-0-ß-D-glucopyranoside (VII),
kaempferol (VIII) and kaempferol 3-0-a-L-rhamnoside (IX). The structures of I—VI were
established on the basis of chemical and spectroscopic data to be 2,5-dimethyl-7-
hydroxychromone (I), 2-methyl-5-acetonyl-7-hydroxychromone (II), 2-methyI-5-carboxymethyl-
7-hydroxychromone (III), 2-(2'-hydroxypropyl)-5-methyl-7-hydroxychromone (I V), 2-(2'-
hydroxypropyl)-5-methyI-7-hydroxychromone 7-0-ß-D-gIucopyranoside (V) and 2-methyI-5-
carboxymethyl-7-hydroxychromanone (VI).
Keywords—rhubarb; Polygonaceae; chromone; chromanone; naphthalene derivative;
flavonoid
In previous papers, the isolation of a variety of phenolic glycosides, i.e., anthra-
quinones,2) naphthalenes,l) stilbenes,3) flavanoids,4) and tannins and related compounds,5'6)
from various rhubarbs has been reported. As a continuation of our studies on rhubarb, we
have investigated phenolic constituents of a representative of rhubarb (commercial name:
and have isolated five chromones (I—V), and a chromanone (VI), together with
the known naphthalene glucoside (VII) and flavonoids (VIII and IX). This paper deals with
the structure elucidation of these compounds.
The isolation of compounds I—IX from the aqueous acetone extracts was achieved by
repeated chromatography on Sephadex LH-20, MCI-gel CHP-20P and silica gel using a
variety of solvent systems (Chart l).
Compounds VII, VIII and IX were identified as torachrysone 8-0-ß-D-glucopyranoside,
kaempferol and kaempferol 3-0-1-L-rhamnoside, respectively, by comparisons of their
physical and spectral data with those described in the literature.1 7)
