     Natural products of ﬁlamentous fungi: enzymes, genes, and their regulation

       Dirk Hoffmeister*a and Nancy P. Kellerb

          Received (in Cambridge, UK) 30th August 2006
           First published as an Advance Article on the web 20th December 2006
        DOI: 10.1039/b603084j


          Covering: up to June 2006

       We review the literature on the enzymes, genes, and whole gene clusters underlying natural product
          biosyntheses and their regulation in ﬁlamentous fungi. We have included literature references from
          1958, yet the majority of citations are between 1995 and the present. A total of 295 references are cited.
