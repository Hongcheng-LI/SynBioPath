   Methylated VLPs Q–T (1–4) in companion with their corresponding
non-methylated counterparts VLPs A, B (5),  I (6) and C were then
evaluated for anti-inﬂuenza A virus as well as anti-MRSA activities. As
shown in Table 3, all the VLPs showed considerable anti-H1N1 and
anti-H3N2   activities  with  IC50  values  of  30.6–132.4 μM  and

in Table 4 and Fig. 4, methylation at 4-OH led to decreased (2) or
