   Furthermore, photoenzymatic catalysis has gained traction as a
sustainable alternative to traditional catalytic processes, utilizing light
as a clean energy source to drive enzymatic reactions. The fusion of
photochemistry with enzymology has unlocked novel reaction pathways
previously inaccessible to biological systems. Key advances include the
exploitation of photocatalytic promiscuity of natural enzymes, rational
design of artificial photoenzymes and synergistic photobiocatalysis [9].
Lastly, electro-enzyme catalysis combines enzymatic processes with
electrochemical methods, enabling direct conversion of electrical energy
into chemical energy. This innovative approach has significant impli­
cations for renewable energy applications, such as biofuel cells and
energy storage systems. Recent innovations focus on immobilizing en­
zymes on electrodes to facilitate electron transfer.
   The landscape of enzyme engineering is rapidly evolving, driven by
methodological advancements and the emergence of new catalytic
strategies (Fig. 1). This review aims to provide a comprehensive over­
view of these developments, highlighting the transformative impact of
AI-guided enzyme engineering, chemo-enzyme, photo-enzyme, and
electro-enzyme catalysis on the future of biocatalysis and its role in
addressing global challenges in enzyme engineering.

                                                                        introducing new enzymatic functions.
