           Chalcones display a wide range of biological and pharma-
          cological activities including antibacterial, antifungal, antiviral,
           cytotoxic, antitumor, anti-inammatory and anti-allergic activ-

         isobavachalcone. Xanthohumol has been demonstrated  to
          exhibit anti-HIV-1, antibacterial and antitumor  activities.4–12
