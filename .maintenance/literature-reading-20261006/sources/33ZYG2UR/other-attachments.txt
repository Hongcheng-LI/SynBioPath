1. Experimental Procedures 
1. 1 Strains and general DNA manipulation techniques 
Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4 was used to get cDNA cultivated in YES medium (2% Yeast 
exact, 15% Sucrose, 0.05% MgSO4·7H2O, 0.0005% CuSO4·5H2O, 0.001% ZnSO4·7H2O, 2% Agar). 
Saccharomyces cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA was used as the yeast expression host, which cultivated in 
YPD medium (1% Yeast exact, 2% Glucose, 2% Peptone). E. coli XL-1 was used for cloning, 
following standard recombinant DNA techniques. E. coli BL21(DE3) and E. coli BAP1 were used 
for protein expression. A. nidulans (Δors-BGC strain) was used as the host for AN7909 
overexpression. Acremonium sp. Mcr was used to get cDNA of gene AcsA cultivated in PDA 
medium (2.6% Potato Dextrose Water, 2% agar). DNA restriction enzymes were used as 
recommended by the manufacturer (New England Biolabs, NEB). PCR was performed using Q5® 
High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase (NEB). PCR products were confirmed by DNA sequencing.  
1.2 Plasmid construction 
The primers used in this study are listed in Table S1. The plasmids are summarized in Table 
S2. For expression of AN7909, AN7910 and AN7911 in S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA, pIM 51, pIM 
52 and pIM 53 were constructed with the gene AN7909, AN7910 and AN7911 amplified from cDNA 
of Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4. These genes are inserted into vectors pYEU, pYET and pYEL 
using the primer pair pYEU-AN7909-F1/pYEU-AN7909-R1/pYEU-AN7909-F2/pYEU-AN7909-
R2, pYETAN7910-F/pYET-AN7910-R and pYEL-AN7911-F/pYEL-AN7911-R respectively. 
For expression of AN7909-ACP1(S1676A) and AN7909-ACP2(S1775A) in S. cerevisiae 
BJ5464-NpgA, pIM 54 and pIM 55 were constructed with the gene AN7909 ACP1(S1676A) and 
AN7909 ACP2(S1775A) cloned from AN7909 Ser1676 and Ser1775 mutants. These genes are 
inserted into vector pYEU using the primer pairs ACP1S-F/ ACP1S-R/pYEU-AN7909-F1/pYEU-
AN7909-R1/pYEU-AN7909-F2/pYEU-AN7909-R2, ACP2S-F/ACP2S-R/pYEUAN7909F1/pYEU-
AN7909-R1/pYEU-AN7909F2/pYEUAN7909-R2 respectively. 
For expression of AcsA in S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA, pIM 56 was constructed with the gene 
AcsA cloned from Acremonium sp. Mcr cDNA. It is inserted into vectors pYEU using the primer 
pairs pYEU- AcsA-F/ pYEU- AcsA-R. 
To express AN7910 and AN7911 in E. coli BL21(DE3), the gene AN7910 and AN7911 were 
cloned from the cDNA of Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4 using the primer pairs pCold I-AN7910-
F/pCold I-AN7910-R and pCold I-AN7911-F/pCold I-AN7911-R respectively, then the fragments 
were cloned into vector pCold I to generate pIM 57and pIM 58. 
To express AN7910 (H68A) and AN7911(R16A) in E. coli BL21(DE3), which cloned from the 
AN7910 mutant His68 and AN7910 mutant Arg16 using the primer pair pCold I-AN7910-F/pCold 
I-AN7910-R/Pcold-AN7910H68A-F/Pcold-AN7910H68A-R and pCold I-AN7910-F/pCold I-
AN7910-R/ Pcold-AN7910R16A-F/ Pcold-AN7910R16A-R respectively, then the fragments were 
cloned into vector pCold I to generate pIM 59 and pIM 60. 
To construct the plasmids for overexpression of AN7909 in A. nidulans (Δors-BGC strain), 
which cloned from the cDNA of Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4 using the primer pairs pANR-
AN7909-F/pYEU-AN7909-R1/pANR-AN7909-R/pYEU-AN7909-F2, then the fragments were 
cloned into vector pANR to generate pIM 61. 
To express AN7909-TE mutant (S1907A) and single AN7909-TE domain in S. cerevisiae

1.3 Phylogenetic tree analyses of i) the SnoaL fold protein and AN7910; ii) KS 
domain of orsellinic acid synthase and AN7909 
Multiple sequence alignment of proteins i) the SnoaL fold protein and AN7910; ii) KS domain 
of orsellinic acid synthase and AN7909. All proteins from NCBI database were performed using 
ClustalW. The evolutionary analyses were conducted in MEGA7 software and the phylogenetic tree 
was inferred by using neighbor-joining method.  
1.4 Isolation of the cDNA from Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4, and reverse 
transcription PCR (RT-PCR) 
Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4 was grown on YES medium at 37 °C for 4 days. The mycelia 
were collected and frozen at -80°C. The mycelia were then placed in a mortar with liquid nitrogen 
and ground into powder. The RNA was obtained by using Fungal RNA Kit (OMEGA) and the cDNA 
was synthesized using the Transcriptor cDNA Synthesis Kit (Roche). 
1.5 Proteins expression and purification 
The recombinant expression plasmids pIM 57, pIM 58, pIM 59 and pIM 60 were transformed 
into E. coli BL21 (DE3) respectively and cultivated in 1 L LB medium at 37 °C with 100 mg/L 
Ampicillin (Amp). When the OD600 reached 0.4-0.6, The cultures were then incubated on 16 °C for 
30 min then 0.2 mM IPTG (final concentration) was added to the cultures, which were then 
cultivated for an additional 19-20 h at 16°C. The cells were harvested and resuspended in 20 mL 
buffer A (50 mM Tris-HCl, pH 7.5, 0.5 M NaCl, 10% glycerol) and lysed by sonication on ice for 
10 min. Cellular debris was removed by centrifugation (12000 g, 30 min, 4°C). His-tagged proteins 
(AN7910, AN7911, AN7910(H68A), AN7910(R16A) were purified by Ni-NTA affinity 
chromatography and were eluted with an increasing concentration of imidazole in buffer A. The 
purified protein was passed through a PD-10 desalting column (GE Healthcare) and eluted with 
buffer C (50 mM Tris-Cl, 50 mM NaCl, 5% glycerol, pH 7.5). Concentration of protein was gained 
after using 10 kDa ultrafiltration centrifugal tube (Millipore Amicon ® Ultra-15 mL) at 4 °C, 3750 
rpm, and the protein was quickly frozen in liquid nitrogen and saved at -80 °C. The purified enzymes 
were checked by SDS-PAGE, and the concentrated enzymes were checked by BCA Protein 
quantification kit (Beijing Dingguo Changsheng Biotechnology Co., Ltd). 
1.6 Biochemical analyses of AN7910, AN7910(R16A), AN7910(H68A) In vitro 
Biochemical analyses of enzymes (AN7910, AN7910(R16A), AN7910(H68A)) were 
performed in the 100 µL Tris-HCl buffer (pH 7.5) containing 500 µM substrate (compound 2 or 
compound 8) and 10 µM enzyme. The reaction mixtures were incubated at 28 °C for 2 hours and 
extracted with 100 µL ethyl acetate (1% (v/v) CH3COOH). The ethyl acetate (1% (v/v) CH3COOH) 
phase was obtained after centrifugation and was dried with centrifugal concentrator. Then the 
samples were analyzed by HPLC. 
1.7 Biochemical analyses of AN7911 in vitro 
The activity assay of AN7911 was performed in the 100 µL Tris-HCl buffer (pH 7.5) 
containing 500 uM substrates (compound 3, 4, a, b, c, d, e) and 10 µM AN7911. The reaction 
mixtures were incubated at 28 °C for 2 hours and extracted with 100 µL ethyl acetate (1%

1.8 Heterologous expression of diorcinol gene cluster (ors) and AcsA in S. cerevisiae 
BJ5464-NpgA 
According to the experimental design, the recombinant S. cerevisiae expression plasmids pIM 
51 (BJ-AN7909); pIM 51 and pIM 52 (BJ-AN7909-10); pIM 51 and pIM 53 (BJ-AN7909-11); pIM 
51, pIM 52 and pIM 53 (BJ-AN7909-10-11); pIM 54 (BJ-AcsA); pIM 55 (BJ-AN7909-ACP1m); pIM 
56 (BJ-AN7909-ACP2m); pIM 62 (BJ-AN7909-TEm); pIM 62 and pIM 63 (BJ-AN7909-TEm and 
trans-TE) were transformed into S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA by using EasyCompTM Transformation 
Kit (Invitrogen), respectively. The cells were firstly grown in a minimal medium lacking uracil, 
tryptophan or leucine for 2 days. The seed culture (2.5 mL) was transferred into 25 mL YPD media, 
and then incubated at 28°C, 250 rpm for 3 days. The supernatant was extracted with equal volume 
of ethyl acetate (1%(v/v) CH3COOH). The organic extract was combined, concentrated and then 
dissolved in 200 μL methanol for HPLC analysis.  
1.9 Preparation of A. nidulans (Δors-BGC strain) protoplast 
The spores of A. nidulans (Δors-BGC strain) were inoculated into 25 mL were germinated in 
liquid CD medium (1 L: 10 g glucose, 50 mL 20 × nitrate salts, 1 mL trace elements, pH 6.5) 
containing 10 mM uridine, 5 mM uracil, 0.5 μg/mL pyridoxine HCl and 0.125 μg/mL riboflavin at 
37 °C about 9 h. The mycelia were harvested by centrifugation at 3,750 rpm for 5 min at 4 °C, 
washed three times with 15 mL Osmotic buffer (1.2 M MgSO4·7H2O, 10 mM sodium phosphate, 
pH 5.8). Then the mycelia were transferred into a sterile 50 mL Erlenmeyer flask containing 30 mg 
lysing enzymes from Trichoderma and 20 mg Yatalase in 10 mL of Osmotic buffer. The flask was 
shaked at 28 °C, 80 rpm overnight, and the cells were poured directly in a sterile 50 mL centrifugal 
tube and overlaid gently with 10 mL of Trapping buffer (0.6 M sorbitol, 0.1 M Tris-HCl, pH 7.0). 
Finally, the protoplasts were obtained and resuspended in STC buffer (1.2 M sorbitol, 10 mM CaCl2, 
10 mM Tris-HCI, pH 7.5) for transformation after centrifugation at 5000 rpm for 20 min at 4 °C.  
1.10 Overexpression of AN7909 in A. nidulans (Δors-BGC strain) 
The recombinant plasmids pIM 61 used for overexpression of AN7909 was dissolved in STC 
buffer, added to the protoplasts of A. nidulans (Δors-BGC strain). The mixture was grown on the 
regeneration dropout solid medium (CD medium with 1.2 mM sorbitol and appropriate supplements) 
at 37 °C about 2 days. The transformants were incubated in liquid CD-ST production medium (20 
g/L starch, 10 g/L peptone, 50 mL/L nitrate salts, 1 mL/L trace elements) at 25 °C, 220 rpm, 3 days, 
while the products were extracted and analyzed by HPLC. 
1.11 Biochemical characterization of AN7909-TE mutant 
S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA :: pIM 62 (BJ-AN7909-TEm) and S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA were 
cultured in 10 ml YPD at 28 °C for 72 hours. Then the cells were collected by centrifugation to 
remove the supernatant. The cells were grinded to powders in liquid nitrogen, then 900 µL Tris-HCl 
buffer (pH 7.5) was added to become crude enzyme solution. The alkaline hydrolysis assays were 
performed at 1 mL scale in the presence of 0.1 M NaOH (final concentration) and 900 µL crude enzyme 
solution incubated at 75°C for 15 min. Then the solution was adjusted to pH 7.5 by CH3COOH and 
extracted with 1 ml ethyl acetate (1% (v/v) CH3COOH). The organic phase was separated, dried, 
dissolved in 100 µl methanol and analyzed by LC-MS.

1.12 Sample analysis, compound isolation and structure elucidation 
LC-MS analyses were conducted on a Waters ACQUITY CH-A UPLC-MS with a PDA 
detector and a SQD2 mass detector (ACQUITY UPLC® BEH, 1.7 m, 2.1 × 100 mm, C18 column) 
using negative ionization. Water (solvent A with 0.02 % formic acid (v/v)) and acetonitrile (solvent 
B with 0.02 % formic acid (v/v)) were used at a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min. Samples were analyzed 
with a linear gradient of 5%-99 % solvent B for 8 minutes followed by 99 % solvent B for another 
4 minutes. 
HPLC analysis was conducted on Agilent Technologies 1260 Infinity with a UV detector 
(Agilent 5 mm, 4.6 × 250 mm, C18 column). Water (solvent A with 0.05 % formic acid (v/v)) and 
acetonitrile (solvent B) were used at a flow rate of 1 mL/min. Samples were analyzed with a linear 
gradient of 5%-95% solvent B in 0.0 min-15.0 min, 95% solvent B in 15.1 min-20.0 min, 5%  
solvent B in 20.1 min-25.0 min. 
S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA :: pIM 51(BJ-AN7909) was cultured in 1 L YPD at 28 °C for 72 
hours. The supernatant and cells were extracted three times with ethyl acetate (1% (v/v) CH3COOH) 
respectively. The extracts were combined and evaporated to dryness to yield 0.8 g crude extract. 
The 0.8 g crude extract was firstly separated by preparative HPLC (Hitachi® preparative HPLC; 
YMC ODS-A 5 μm 120A 10×250 mm C18 column) with a linear gradient of 10-90 % (v/v) MeOH 
in H2O (0.05% (v/v) formic acid) at a flow rate of 2 mL/min to yield crude compound 2 (25 mg), 
crude compound 6 (18 mg), crude compound 7 (12 mg) and purified compound 8 (25 mg). The 
crude compounds 2 and 6 were then separated by preparative HPLC with MeOH-H2O (0.05% (v/v) 
formic acid) (45:55) to yield the purified compounds 2 (12 mg) and 6 (8 mg). The crude compound 
7 (12 mg) was then separated by preparative HPLC with MeOH-H2O (0.05% (v/v) formic acid) 
(60:40) to yield the purified compound 7 (6 mg). 
S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA :: pIM 51 and pIM 52 (BJ-AN7909-10) was cultured on 1 L YPD 
at 28 °C for 72 hours. The supernatant and cells were extracted three times with ethyl acetate (1% 
(v/v) CH3COOH). The extracts were combined and evaporated to dryness to yield 0.78 g crude 
extract. The 0.78 g crude extract was separated by preparative HPLC with with a linear gradient of 
10-90 % (v/v) methanol in H2O (0.05% (v/v) formic acid) at a flow rate of 2 mL/min to yield the 
purified compounds 3 (20 mg) and 9 (15 mg). 
S. cerevisiae BJ5464-NpgA :: pIM 51, pIM 52 and pIM 53 (BJ-AN7909-10-11) was cultured 
on 1 L YPD at 28 °C for 72 hours.The supernatant and cells were extracted three times with ethyl 
acetate (1% (v/v) CH3COOH). The extracts were combined and evaporated to dryness to yield 0.67 
g crude extract. The 0.67 g crude extract was firstly separated by preparative HPLC with a linear 
gradient of 10-90 % (v/v) MeOH in H2O (0.05% (v/v) formic acid) at a flow rate of 2 mL/min to 
yield crude compound 1 (20 mg). The crude compound 1 (20 mg) was separated by preparative 
HPLC with MeOH-H2O (0.05% (v/v) formic acid) (40:60) to yield the purified compound 1 (12 
mg).
