   Structure of bifunctional variediene synthase
   yields unique insight on biosynthetic
   diterpene assembly and cyclization




        Received: 16 December 2024                 Eliott S. Wenger  & David W. Christianson

       Accepted: 28 May 2025

                                  An unusual family of bifunctional terpene synthases has been identiﬁed in
                                        which a prenyltransferase assembles 5-carbon precursors to form C20 ger-

                                             anylgeranyl diphosphate (GGPP), which is then converted into a polycyclic
                                         product by a cyclase. Here, we report the cryo-EM structure of a massive,1234567890():,;                 1234567890():,;                                     495-kD bifunctional terpene synthase, variediene synthase from Emericella
                                                variecolor (EvVS). The structure reveals a hexameric prenyltransferase core
                                        sandwiched between two triads of cyclases. Surprisingly, GGPP is not chan-
                                          neled intramolecularly from the prenyltransferase to the cyclase, but instead is

                                               also report the identiﬁcation of bifunctional terpene synthases in an animal,
                                           Adineta steineri, a bdelloid rotifer indigenous to freshwater environments.
