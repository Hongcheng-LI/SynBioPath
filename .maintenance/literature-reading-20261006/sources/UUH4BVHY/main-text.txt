              ary metabolite. Surprisingly, this strain also produced a trace amount of echinomycin. We                                                                    Terms

                 this 36 kilobase-long gene cluster contained an ORF homologous to ecm18 that encodes a
              thioacetal-forming enzyme responsible for the triostin A-to-echinomycin bioconversion. These                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      Conditions
             ﬁndings indicate that, unlike previously thought, S. triostinicus is capable of producing not
              only triostin A but also echinomycin. Our observation suggests potential value in careful re-
               analysis for metabolites from previously characterized natural product producers with the
              current technologies.
