Abstract

A rapid and credible analytical method was developed using online UPLC-ESI-Q-TOF-MS/MS to iden-
tify chemical constituents in Polygoni cuspidati folium and its preparation. By accurate mass measure-
ments within 6.5 ppm error for [M–H]−ion in routine analysis, 26 chemical constituents, including
tannin, derivatives of phenylpropionic acid, stilbene, ﬂavonoid, anthraquinone, torachryson and its de-rivatives, were identiﬁed or tentatively characterized.
