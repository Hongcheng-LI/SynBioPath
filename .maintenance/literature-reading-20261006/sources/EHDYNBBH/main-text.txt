                                                           3. Conclusion

                                                   Our research has established that S-TeA is a novel natural prod-
                                                                  uct synthesized by a variety of ﬁlamentous fungi. Chiral HPLC
                                                      and ECD analyses conﬁrmed the absolute conﬁguration of Nat-
                                                          S-TeA and its relationship to structural and bioactive proper-
                                                                                   ties. Additionally, we identiﬁed threonine as a precursor, along
                                                             with nine key biosynthetic genes and the corresponding metabo-
                                                                                  lites they produce for S-TeA synthesis in A. alternata, conﬁrm-
                                                              ing the biosynthetic mechanism. Despite sharing threonine as a

                                                  AaMFS1, the biosynthetic pathway of S-TeA diﬀers from that of
                                                    TeA due to the involvement of three unique enzymes, IPMS, IP-                                          MDH, and ISMD.
