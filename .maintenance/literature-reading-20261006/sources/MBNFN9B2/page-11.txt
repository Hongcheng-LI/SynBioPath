Screening and conﬁrmation of gene-replacement
mutants
The U. virens wild-type and mutant strains were cultured in liq-
uid PS medium at 28C with shaking at 150 r.p.m. for 5 days
and the mycelia were then collected for genomic DNA extrac-
tion via the cetyltrimethylammonium bromide method as
described (Liu et al., 2013). To screen the UvPKS1 gene-
replacement mutants, the primer sets hph-F/R and UvPKS1-
F/R were used to amplify the hph and UvPKS1 genes,
respectively. The primers UvPKS1-P2-F/hph-P2-R and hph-
P3-F/ UvPKS1-P3-R were used to rule out the ectopic trans-
formants with random T-DNA insertional events (Supporting
Information Fig. S1 and Table S1). The primers for diagnostic
PCR to screen ugsO, ugsJ, ugsL, ugsR2 and ugsT replace-
ment mutants are also listed in Supporting Information
Table S1.
Southern blot analysis was performed as described previ-
ously (Menke et al., 2012). The probes were ampliﬁed from
the pGKO2-hyg constructs or fusion PCR products with
primers listed in Supporting Information Table S1, and were
then labelled with DIG High Prime DNA Labeling Kit following
the manufacturer’s instructions (Roche, Germany). Genomic
DNA (4–6 μg) isolated from the wild-type and mutant strains
was digested with the indicated restriction enzymes and was
separated by electrophoresis in 0.7% agarose gel. The gel
was equilibrated in 20 × SSC buffer (3 M NaCl, 0.3 M
C6H5Na3O7, pH 7.0) and DNA was blotted onto positively
charged nylon membrane (GE Healthcare, UK). After cross-
linking, the membranes were subsequently incubated over-
night with DIG-labelled probes. The signals on the mem-
branes were detected using the Detection Starter Kit (Roche,
Germany) according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
Gene expression analyses
Total RNAs were isolated from U. virens mycelia harvested
at 7 days after culturing in PS liquid medium using the TRIzol
reagent following the manufacturer’s instructions (Invitrogen,
Carlsbad, CA, USA). Reverse transcription (RT) was per-
formed with the Reverse Transcription System (Takara,
Dalian, China) using the oligo-d(T) primer. RT-PCR was per-
formed using the primers listed in Supporting Information
Table S1 to detect expression of different ugs genes. The
expression of α-tubulin gene was detected as a reference
using the primer pair α-tubulin-F/R (Gu et al., 2012).
Complementation analyses
The full-length ugsO, ugsT, ugsJ and ugsL genes with
their native promoter sequences (~1.5 kb) were ampliﬁed

Chemical analysis of ustilaginoidin derivatives and
intermediates in U. virens
For chemical analyses, the wild-type, mutant and comple-
mentary strains were grown on PSA plates at 28C for
4 weeks. The hyphae were then collected and incubated
with EtOAc at room temperature for 24 h with three
EtOAc changes. The EtOAc extracts were combined and
dried under vacuum at 40C on a rotatory evaporator.
The residue was dissolved in methanol and ﬁltered
through a microporous ﬁlter (pore size, 0.22 μm). The
sample (10 μl) was loaded on an Agilent ZORBAX SB-
C18 column (5 μm, 150 × 4.6 mm; Agilent Technologies,
Polo Alto, CA, USA) and was then eluted using an Agi-
lent 1290 Inﬁnity UHPLC system (Agilent, Waldbronn,
Germany) at 30C with the ﬂow rate of 1 ml/min under a
gradient solvent program as follows: 0–2 min, 50% meth-
anol; 2–12 min, 50%–70% methanol; 12–27 min, 70%–
90% methanol; 27–52 min, increased to 99% methanol;
52–55 min, 100% methanol. The eluates were detected with
UV at 290 nm (at 280 nm for the metabolites from ΔugsL).
