                                             of fungal species and play a key role for virulence of numerous pathogenic
                                                 fungi. Despite their biological signiﬁcance, our understanding of how these
                                                iron-chelating cyclic hexapeptides are assembled by non-ribosomal peptide
                                            synthetase (NRPS) enzymes remains poorly understood, primarily due to the
                                               nonlinearity exhibited by the domain architecture. Herein, we report the
                                           biochemical characterization of the SidC NRPS, responsible for construction
                                             of the intracellular siderophore ferricrocin. In vitro reconstitution of puriﬁed
                                         SidC reveals its ability to produce ferricrocin and its structural variant, ferri-
                                        chrome. Application of intact protein mass spectrometry uncovers several
                                            non-canonical events during peptidyl siderophore biosynthesis, including
                                            inter-modular loading of amino acid substrates and an adenylation domain
                                           capable of poly-amide bond formation. This work expands the scope of NRPS
                                       programming, allows biosynthetic assignment of ferrichrome NRPSs, and sets
                                           the stage for reprogramming towards novel hydroxamate scaffolds.
