UNIV          Filamentous fungi produce a diverse array of bioactive secondary
            metabolites. Among the small molecule natural products, polyketides
          and nonribosomal peptides are synthesized by multidomain enzymesOCEAN          such as polyketide synthases (PKSs) and nonribosomal peptide
via         synthetases (NRPSs), respectively. The enzymology and biochemi-
            cal properties of these megaenzymes are highly complex and are
            different from the well-studied bacterial PKSs and NRPSs.1 In
