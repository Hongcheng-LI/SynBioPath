  The frequent occurrence of laccase-encoding genes in BGCs
of dimeric polyketides suggests a common, widespread, lac-
case-based dimerization system. However, in some cases, the
composition of the putative polyketide dimer BGCs suggests
an involvement of FAS protein in the phenol-coupling step.
This observation  is supported by knockouts of FAS proteins
identified in the BGCs of aurofusarin in Fusarium graminearum
and 13 in C. beticola.[25,29] In the case of aurofusarin, knockout
of the FAS protein encoding gene aurS abolished aurofusarin
production, whereas  its monomeric  precursor 9 was   still
detected.[25] Similarly, no dimer production was observed in
DCbCTB11 mutants of C. beticola.[29] Although FAS protein en-
coding genes were identified in the putative BGCs of the 8,8’-
linked dimers 2, 7, and aurofusarin, the BGCs of 6,6’-linked 3
(and likely 5) did not contain such a gene. This suggests an
