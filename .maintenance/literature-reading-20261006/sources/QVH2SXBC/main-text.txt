  To better deﬁne the ordering of intermediates of the
pathway, a cell-free biosynthetic approach was taken.
Because the initial oxygenation step of the pathway,
the hydroxylation of taxa-4(5),11(12)-diene to taxa-
4(20),11(12)-dien-5a-ol, is mediated by a microsomal,
cytochrome P450 mixed function oxidase (15), and be-
cause in vitro (10) evidence suggests that subsequent
oxygenation steps are also catalyzed by cytochrome
P450 enzymes, induced T. cuspidata cell microsomal
preparations, isolated with the necessary precautions
(25) and optimized for the hydroxylation of taxadiene
(15), were employed to examine the biosynthetic con-
versions of taxadien-5a-ol and the corresponding ace-
tate ester.
  Incubation of these microsomal preparations with 30
mM taxoid substrate, in the presence of saturating
levels of NADPH (2 mM) and molecular oxygen, gave
rise to a complex set of polyoxygenated products in
both cases (30–40% conversion of substrate) as re-
vealed by radio-HPLC analysis of the extracts (Fig. 3).

metabolite (peaks 2–10 of Fig. 3A) was isolated by
preliminary reversed-phase HPLC and subjected to
