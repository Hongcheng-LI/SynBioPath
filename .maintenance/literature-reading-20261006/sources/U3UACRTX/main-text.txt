Keywords:                                       3-Carene is a bicyclic monoterpene with a pine aroma. It is widely used as a ﬂavoring agent, food additive,
3-carene                                    and insecticide in food and agricultural applications. Therefore, establishing an eﬃcient microbial pathway for
Saccharomyces cerevisiae                            3-carene is important. At present, the low activity of 3-carene synthase (CarTS) and the insuﬃcient supply of
NPP orthogonal pathway                            precursors in Saccharomyces cerevisiae signiﬁcantly constrain the synthesis of 3-carene. In this study, the NPP
Truncation of enzyme
                                               orthogonal pathway, source screening, CarTS modiﬁcation, enzymatic subcellular localization, and molecular
Subcellular localization
molecular chaperones                            chaperone screening were used to promote 3-carene synthesis. By introducing the NPP orthogonal pathway and
                                                 truncated CarTS from Salvia stenophylla ( Ss CarTS), the yield of 3-carene increased by 32.1-fold. Subsequently,
                                              through enzymatic subcellular localization and molecular chaperone screening, the yield of 3-carene was in-
                                                creased by 39.0%, reaching 50.6 mg/L in shake ﬂask cultures. Eventually, the ﬁnal titer of 3-carene reached
                                             506.7 mg/L in a 5-L bioreactor by optimizing the culture medium containing metal ions and vitamins, which is
                                                 the highest reported titer of 3-carene in microbial cell factories. In this study, we provide a cell factory for the
                                                    synthesis of 3-carene as well as reference data for the biosynthesis of other bicyclic monoterpenes.
