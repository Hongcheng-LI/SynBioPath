        We dene pericyclases as those enzymes that catalyze pericyclic
           reactions, reactions that have transition states with bonds partially
       made or broken in a cyclic array. It is this cyclic array of bonding
         changes in the rate-determining transition state that is special for
         a pericyclic reaction, rather than the detailed shape of the potential
           surface. A time gap between formation of the two new bonds of <60
            fs is the denition of dynamically concerted, but we consider
          reactions as pericyclic as long as the reaction is energetically
          concerted, with a rate-determining TS that has a cyclic array of
         bonding interactions that eventually lead to products.
