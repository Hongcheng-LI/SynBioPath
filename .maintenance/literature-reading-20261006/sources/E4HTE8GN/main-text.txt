ABSTRACT: Seven new 4-hydroxy-6-phenyl-2H-pyran-2-one
(HPPO) derived meroterpenoids, 1-methyl-12a,12b-epoxyarisuga-
cin M (1), 1-methyl-4a,12b-epoxyarisugacin M (2), 2,3-dihydroxy-
3,4a-epoxy-12a-dehydroxyisoterreulactone A (3), 2-hydroxy-12a-
dehydroxyisoterreulactone A (4), 3′-demethoxyterritrems B′ (5),
4a-hydroxyarisugacin P (6), and 1-epi-arisugacin H (7), together
with two known analogues (8 and 9), were isolated from the
marine-derived fungal strain Penicillium sp. SCSIO 41691. Their
structures were elucidated by spectroscopic methods, and the
absolute configurations of compounds 1 and 3 were determined by
single-crystal X-ray diffraction. Among them, 1 and 2 had a unique
methyl migration in the basic meroterpenoid skeleton with a
12a,12b-epoxy or 4a,12b-epoxy group, and 3 was a highly
oxygenated HPPO-derived meroterpenoid featuring a rare 6/5/6/6/6/6 hexacyclic system with a 3,4a-epoxy group. Biologically,
5 exhibited inhibitory activity against lipopolysaccharide-induced nitric oxide production in RAW 264.7 cells with an IC50 value of 21
μM, more potent than the positive control indomethacin.
M
eroterpenoids are widely distributed in nature.1 In a
recent review about the chemistry and biology of fungal
meroterpenoids between 2008−2019,2 meroterpenoids were
divided into 12 species according to their nonterpenoid parts.
Among these, meroterpenoids derived from 4-hydroxy-6-
phenyl-2H-pyran-2-one (HPPO), though relatively few in
number, exhibited remarkable bioactivities, such as anti-
inflammation3 and AChE inhibitory activity.4,5 Representative
examples include the territrems6−9 and arisugacins.4,10−12
Marine-derived fungi are widely recognized as prolific
sources of structurally unique and biologically active natural
products. In order to obtain novel meroterpenoids from fungi,
we screened extracts from our fungal strain library by LCMS
and found that the fungal strain Penicillium sp. SCSIO 41691
isolated from a South China Sea soft coral could produce
meroterpenoids. Our further investigation of the secondary
metabolites of this strain led to the obtainment of seven new
HPPO-derived meroterpenoids, 1-methyl-12a,12b-epoxyarisu-
gacin M (1), 1-methyl-4a,12b-epoxyarisugacin M (2), 2,3-
dihydroxy-3,4a-epoxy-12a-dehydroxyisoterreulactone A (3), 2-
hydroxy-12a-dehydroxyisoterreulactone A (4), 3′-demethox-
yterritrems B′ (5), 4a-hydroxyarisugacin P (6), and 1-epi-
arisugacin H (7), together with two known analogues
territrems B′ and A′ (8 and 9).13 Compounds 1−9 were
tested to evaluate their inhibitory activity against nitric oxide
(NO) production in lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced RAW
264.7 cells and their cytotoxicity against the human lung
adenocarcinoma cell line H1975.

Detailed analysis of HSQC, HMBC, and COSY spectra
(Figure 1) proved that 1 had a similar skeleton to that of
arisugacin M. However, the COSY correlations of H-1 (δH
2.94, 1H, ddd, J = 13.8, 7.2, 4.0 Hz) with H3-16 (δH 1.01, 3H,

H3-16 to C-1 (δC 27.1), C-2 (δC 36.1) and C-12b (δC 72.1),
suggested the methyl group at C-12b in the A-ring migrated to
the position of C-1. The chemical shifts of OH-3 (δH 5.46) and
OH-4a (δH 5.69) were assigned on the basis of the HMBC
correlations of δH 5.46 with C-3 and δH 5.69 with C-4a (Figure
1). The molecular formula showing 12 degrees of unsaturation
combined with the chemical shifts of C-12a (δC 66.6) and C-
12b (δC 72.1) suggested the existence of a 12a,12b-epoxy ring.
Thus, the planar structure of 1 was established, as shown.
The configuration of 1 was further determined by the
NOESY spectrum (Figure 2) and a single-crystal X-ray
diffraction experiment. The NOE correlations of H3-16 with
H3-15/H-2β/H-3 and H3-14 with H-3 suggested that H3-16,
H3-15, H3-14 and H-3 were β-oriented. Meanwhile, the NOE
correlations of H3-13 with H-2α revealed that H3-13 was α-
oriented. Furthermore, the absolute configuration of 1 was
further determined as 1R,3R,4aR,6aR,12aR,12bS by single-
crystal X-ray diffraction analysis using Cu Kα radiation (Figure
3), and the calculated ECD curve of 1 was in good agreement
with the experimental one, as shown in Figure 4. To the best of
our knowledge, the unique 6/3/6/6/6/6 polycyclic system
found in 1 is a first among the HPPO-derived meroterpenoids.

1-Methyl-4a,12b-epoxyarisugacin M (2) was found to have
the same molecular formula C27H32O7 as that of 1 by
HRESIMS. The 1H and 13C NMR data of 2 (Table 1) were
very similar to those of 1 with some chemical shifts slightly
varied in the A/B/C/D ring. Detailed analysis of the HMBC
and COSY correlations (Figure 1) proved that 2 had the same
skeleton as 1. The HMBC correlations from H3-16 to C-1, C-2
and C-12b, from OH-12a to C-12, C-12a and C-12b, from H2-
5 to C-4a, and from H3-14 to C-4 and C-4a, suggested an
4a,12b-epoxy ring replaced the 12a,12b-epoxy ring of 1.
Furthermore, the NOE correlations from H3-16 to H-3 and H-
12β, from H3-14 to H-3 and H-5β, and from H3-15 to H-5β
and H-12β confirmed that H3-16 and H3-15 were β-oriented
(Figure 2). On the contrary, the NOE correlations from H3-13
to OH-3 and H-5α revealed that H3-13 and OH-3 were α-
oriented. As 1 and 2 presumably share a biosynthetic pathway,
it was reasonable to infer that OH-12a and the 4a,12b-epoxy
ring in 2 were also α-oriented as those in 1.
2,3-Dihydroxy-3,4a-epoxy-12a-dehydroxyisoterreulactone A
(3) had the molecular formula C27H32O8 deduced from
HRESIMS, indicating 12 degrees of unsaturation. The 1H and
13C NMR data of 3 (Table 1) were similar to those of 12a-
dehydroxyisoterreulactone A,8 except for the additional
presence of one oxymethine carbon (δC 68.2, δH 3.44) and
one deshielded fully substituted carbon (δC 116.4), as well as
the absence of one methylene and one carbonyl carbon.
Detailed analysis of the HMBC and COSY correlations (Figure
1) proved that 3 had the same C/D/E/F ring system as that of
12a-dehydroxyisoterreulactone A. Moreover, the HMBC
correlations from δH 4.71 to C-1, δC 68.2 and δC 116.4, from
δH 7.10 to δC 68.2 and δC 116.4, from H3-16 to C-1, C-4a, C-
12b and C-12a, as well as the COSY correlation of H-1 with δH
3.44, suggested the assignment of OH-2 (δH 4.71), C-2 (δC
68.2), OH-3 (δH 7.10) and C-3 (δH 116.4) and the presence of
two hydroxy groups attached at C-2 and C-3, respectively.
According to the chemical shifts of C-3 and C-4a and the 12
degrees of unsaturation, it was reasonable to infer that C-3 and
C-4a are connected through an oxygen. Thus, the planar
structure of 3 was inferred, as shown.
The configuration of 3 was determined by the NOESY
spectrum (Figure 2) and a single-crystal X-ray diffraction
experiment. The interrelationships of NOE from H-1β to H-2,
from H3-15 to H-6β, and from H3-16 to H-1β, H-5β, H3-14
and H3-15 suggested β-orientation for H-2, H3-15 and H3-16.
Meanwhile, the correlations of H-12a with H-6α and H3-13
with H-5α indicated that they were α-oriented. The single-
crystal X-ray diffraction experiment using Cu Kα radiation with
a Flack parameter of [0.03(5)] (Figure 5) confirmed the
absolute configuration of 3 as 2R,3S,4aS,6aR,12aR,12bR.
Interestingly, 3 was the first highly oxygenated HPPO-derived
meroterpenoid featuring a rare 6/5/6/6/6/6 hexacyclic system
with a 3,4a-epoxy group.
The molecular formula C27H32O8 of 2-hydroxy-12a-dehy-
droxyisoterreulactone A (4) was established by HRESIMS.
The 1H and 13C NMR data of 4 (Table 2) matched well with
those of 12a-dehydroxyisoterreulactone A,8 except for the
additional presence of one oxymethine (δC 70.3, δH 4.96) and
the absence of one methylene. The HMBC correlations from
OH-2 (δH 5.16) to C-1, C-2 (δC 70.3) and C-3 (δC 170.9), as
well as the COSY correlations of H-1 with H-2 (δH 4.96)
(Figure 1), suggested the presence of a hydroxy group at C-2.
In the NOESY spectrum (Figure 2), the NOE correlations of
H-1β with H-2 and H3-16, H-5β with H3-14 and H3-15, and
H-12β with H3-15 and H3-16 implied that H-2, H3-15 and H3-
16 were β-oriented, while the correlations from OH-2 to H-1α,
from H3-13 to H-5α and OH-4a, and from OH-4a to H-12a,
suggested an α-orientation for OH-2, OH-4a and H-12a. Given
the presumed shared biogenesis of 4 and 3, and the high
similarity between their experimental ECD spectra (Figure 6),
we inferred that the absolute configuration of 4 is the same as
that of 3.

4a-Hydroxyarisugacin P (6) had the molecular formula
C27H30O6 as determined by HRESIMS. The 1H and 13C NMR
data of 6 (Table 3) were highly similar to those of arisugacin
P,4 except for the addition of one oxygenated quaternary
carbon (δC 76.5) and the absence of one methine in 6. The
HMBC correlations from H-1, H3-14 and H3-16 to δC 76.5
(Figure 1) suggested the assignment of C-4a (δC 76.5). The
NOE correlations of OH-4a with H-12a/H-5α and H-5α with
H3-13 indicated that OH-4a and H-12a were α-oriented
(Figure 2). In addition, the NOE correlations of H-5β with H3-
15/H3-16 suggested a β-orientation for H3-15 and H3-16
(Figure 2). Finally, the absolute configuration of 6 was
confirmed to be the same as that of arisugacin P based on the
comparison of their experimental ECD spectra (Figure S60).
1-Epi-arisugacin H (7) was obtained as a white powder with
the molecular formula C29H36O9 determined on the basis of
HRESIMS. The 1H and 13C NMR data of 7 (Table 3) were
very similar to those of arisugacin H,14 and the only obvious
difference between them was the slight chemical shift changes
of some signals. Detailed analysis of the COSY, HSQC and
HMBC spectra of 7 proved that the planar structure of 7 was
the same as that of arisugacin H. Furthermore, in the NOESY
spectrum, the NOE correlations from H3-16 to OH-1, H3-14,
H3-15 and H-3 confirmed that OH-1, H-3, H3-14, H3-15 and
H3-16 were β-oriented. Meanwhile, the NOE correlations from
H-1 to OH-4a and OH-12a, and from OH-12a to OH-4a,
revealed that H-1, OH-4a and OH-12a were α-oriented.
Therefore, 7 was determined as an epimer of arisugacin H at
the C-1 position.

■CONCLUSIONS
In summary, seven new HPPO-derived meroterpenoids, 1-
methyl-12a,12b-epoxyarisugacin M (1), 1-methyl-4a,12b-epox-
yarisugacin M (2), 2,3-dihydroxy-3,4a-epoxy-12a-dehydroxyi-
soterreulactone A (3), 2-hydroxy-12a-dehydroxyisoterreulac-
tone A (4), 3′-demethoxyterritrems B′ (5), 4a-hydroxyar-
isugacin P (6), and 1-epi-arisugacin H (7), together with two
known analogues (8 and 9), were isolated from the marine-
derived fungal strain Penicillium sp. SCSIO 41691. Compounds
1 and 2 exhibited a unique methyl migration in their
meroterpenoid skeleton, along with a 12a,12b-epoxy or
4a,12b-epoxy group, and 3 was a highly oxygenated HPPO-
derived meroterpenoid featuring a rare 6/5/6/6/6/6 hex-
acyclic system with a 3,4a-epoxy group. Biologically, 5
exhibited inhibitory activity against lipopolysaccharide-induced
nitric oxide production in RAW 264.7 cells with an IC50 of 21
μM, comparable to the positive control indomethacin.

General Experimental Procedures. Melting points were
recorded with an SGW X-5 digital display microscopic melting
point instrument. Optical rotations were measured with an Anton
Paar MCP 500 polarimeter. ECD and UV spectra were measured with
an Applied Photophysics Chirascan circular dichroism spectrometer.
The crystallographic data were collected on a Rigaku MicroMax 007
diffractometer equipped with Cu Kα radiation and a graphite
monochromator. IR spectra were obtained on a Shimadzu IR
Affinity-1 Fourier transform infrared spectrophotometer.
1H,
13C

III HD 700 MHz Digital NMR spectrometer and a Bruker AV 500
MHz spectrometer with TMS as reference. HRESIMS data were
obtained on a Bruker MaXis quadrupole-time-of-flight mass
spectrometer. Semipreparative reversed-phase (SP-RP) HPLC was
performed on a Shimadzu LC-20A preparative liquid chromatography
system with a YMC-Pack ODS column (250 × 10 mm, S-5 μm, 12
nm). RP-MPLC (reversed-phase medium-pressure preparative liquid
chromatography) was carried out using the CHEETAH MP200
system (Agela Technologies) and Claricep Flash columns filled with
ODS (40−63 μm, YMC). Sephadex LH-20 (GE Healthcare) was
used for column chromatography (CC). Silica gel (200−300 mesh)
for CC and GF254 for TLC were obtained from Yantai Jiangyou
Silica Gel Development Co., Ltd.
Fungal Material. The fungus Penicillium sp. SCSIO 41691
(GenBank accession number PP112158) was isolated from a soft
coral collected from off the coast of Fiery Cross Reef in the south
China sea (9°39′29.75″ N and 112°59′09.84″ E). The ITS sequence
of this strain showed 99% similarity to that of Penicillium sclerotiorum
KX664361. The strain with a designation number SCSIO 41691 was
deposited in the South China Sea Institute of Oceanology, Chinese
Academy of Science, and its culture collection is glycerol medium.
Fermentation and Extraction. The fungus Penicillium sp.
SCSIO 41691 was grown on a solid autoclaved rice substrate medium
(188 × 1 L Erlenmeyer flasks, each containing 80 g of rice) for 25
days at 26 °C. Afterward, the mycelia were extracted with methanol
three times, and ethyl acetate was used to extract the liquid medium
three times. Then, the extract was combined and evaporated under
reduced pressure to yield an extract (150 g).
Isolation and Purification. The extracts (150 g) were
fractionated by medium-pressure chromatography using a stepped
gradient with petroleum ether (PE)/ethyl acetate (EA) (5 L each of
100:0, 90:10, 80:20, 70:30, 60:40, 50:50, 40:60, 30:70, 20:80, 10:90
and 0:100; v/v) to get eight fractions (Fr.1−Fr.8) based on TLC and
HPLC analysis. Fr.5 was separated with Sephadex LH-20 eluting with
CH2Cl2/MeOH (1:1) to offer subfractions (Fr.5-1−Fr.5-3), and Fr.5-
1 was further purified by MPLC using an ODS column with MeOH/
H2O (v/v 10:90−100:0) to yield ten fractions (Fr.5-1a−Fr.5-1j). Fr.5-
1f was purified using HPLC on a semipreparative C18 column
(MeOH/H2O = 70:30, flow rate = 2 mL/min) to afford 5 (1.5 mg, tR
24.3 min) and 8 (1.3 mg, tR 26.5 min). Compound 6 (2.8 mg, tR 35.8
min) was isolated from Fr.5-1g on a semipreparative C18 column by
HPLC using MeOH-H2O (flow rate = 2.0 mL min−1, 70:30, v/v).
Fr.5-1h was purified using HPLC on a semipreparative C18 column
(MeOH/H2O = 70:30, flow rate = 2 mL/min) to afford 9 (2.8 mg, tR
34.2 min) and Fr.5-1h-a. Subsequently, compound 1 (2.7 mg, tR =
29.0 min) was isolated from Fr.5-1h-a by an analytical C18 column
using CH3CN-H2O (flow rate = 1.0 mL min−1, 40:60, v/v). Fr.6 was
separated with MPLC using an ODS column, eluting with MeOH/
H2O (10:90−100:0, v/v) to offer subfractions (Fr.6-1−Fr.6-9).
Compound 4 (1.0 mg, tR 28.0 min) was isolated from Fr.6-6 on a
semipreparative C18 column by HPLC using MeOH-H2O (flow rate
= 2.0 mL min−1, 70:30, v/v). Fr.6-7 was subjected to a semi-
preparative C18 column by HPLC using CH3CN-H2O (flow rate =
2.0 mL min−1, 44:56, v/v) to yield 2 (1.1 mg, tR 41.0 min) and 7 (2.9
mg, tR 49.0 min). Subsequently, Fr.6-8 was further applied to a
semipreparative C18 column by HPLC using MeOH-H2O (flow rate
= 2.0 mL min−1, 70:30, v/v) to yield 3 (2.5 mg, tR = 35.4 min).
