   Alternaria species are known to produce dimethyl coprogen
siderophores to acquire extracellular iron (Jalal et al., 1988; Jalal
and van der Helm, 1989; Ohra et al., 1995; Oide et al., 2006).
Coprogens contain a diketopiperazine ring (dimerium acid) which
is formed by joining two N5-acyl-N5-hydroxy-ornithine units via a
peptide bond (Jalal et al., 1988; Jalal and van der Helm, 1989).An
additional acyl ornithine unit is attached to the ring via an ester
bond.
