NATURAL PRODUCTS
Getting a handle on peptides
Enhancing the structural diversity of peptide natural products relies on synthetic modifications that are typically
 not chemo- or regioselective. A nonribosomal peptide synthetase has now been engineered to incorporate a
 non-natural amino acid containing a reactive bio-orthogonal handle.

 Figure 1 | Biosynthesis of diketopiperazines (DKPs) using the wild-type or Trp239Ser-engineered nonribosomal peptide synthetase modules GrsA or TycA.
 a, The crystal structure of the wild-type adenylation domain of GrsA (PDB code 1AMU) shows bound l-Phe in red, adenosine monophosphate in yellow and the
 Trp239 active site residue in green. b, The wild-type GrsA–GrsB module pair (or TycA–TycB1 pair) only synthesizes the product d-Phe-l-Pro. c, The Trp239Ser
 mutant can synthesize the propargylated DKP analogues when O-propargyl-l-Tyr is administered to the reaction. Domains: A, adenylation; T, thiolation;
 E, epimerization; and C, condensation.
