Yeast transformation
SC dropout semisynthetic medium was used for the
selection of S. cerevisiae transformants. Transformation
of S. cerevisiae INVSc1-npgA was conducted using
Frozen-EZ Yeast Transformation kit (Zymo Research).
Yeast cells harbouring transformed plasmid(s) were ini-
tially cultured in the SC dropout medium overnight at
30C on a 220 rpm shaker. For expression under GAL
promoter, cells were further cultured for 2 days in SC
medium containing 2% galactose and 1% rafﬁnose in
place of glucose.
