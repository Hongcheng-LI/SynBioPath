                                             We now describe the ﬁrst identiﬁcation of the biosynthetic gene
                                                                          cluster for andrastins, in the publicly available genome database of
                                                                          Penicillium chrysogenum Wisconsin 54-1255.20 The cluster contains
                                                                 eleven genes, and nine of them are likely to be directly involved in
                                                                 the biosynthesis of andrastin A, the most complex andrastin mol-
                                                                          ecule. We characterized a novel terpene cyclase, as well as four
                                                                            tailoring enzymes required for the biosynthesis, by reconstituting
                                                                 the biosynthetic pathway in the fungal heterologous expression
                                                                  host Aspergillus oryzae NSAR1,21 and thus established the complete
                                                                     biosynthetic pathway for andrastin A. This is the ﬁrst report on the
                                                                  heterologous production of andrastin A by the expression of nine
                                                                     genes, and  thus  the  total  biosynthesis  of  andrastin A was
                                                                 accomplished.
