pantothenate either through de novo synthesis or by import from the extracellular environment. In the de
novo route, spermine is oxidized by polyamine oxidase (PAO, Fms1p) to spermidine and 3-aminopropanal;
the latter is converted by aldehyde dehydrogenases (ADH, Ald2p/Ald3p), into β-alanine; β-alanine then

provide pantothenate for CoA biosynthesis. Pantothenate enters a conserved five-step pathway leading
