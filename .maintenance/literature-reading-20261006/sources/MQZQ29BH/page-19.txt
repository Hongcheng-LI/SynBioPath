   Promising avenues for future work include the investigation of additional enzymes in
the CoA biosynthetic pathway beyond PanK as novel drug targets. Structural elucida­
tion of these enzymes through crystallography and computational modeling will be
instrumental in expediting rational design and identification of new drug candidates.
The application of genetic engineering and advanced genomic tools will be critical
for dissecting the functional significance of these enzymes within fungal physiology,
helping to uncover the precise mechanisms by which compounds against these targets
exert their antifungal  effects.  Collectively, such  efforts  will pave the way  for the
development of next-generation antifungal agents equipped to combat the growing
public health burden posed by invasive fungal infections.
