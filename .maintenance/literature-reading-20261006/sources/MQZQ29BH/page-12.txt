biochemical divergence underlies the selective binding of PTZ compounds (fungal

of new drugs targeting fungal PanK. Site-directed mutagenesis confirmed the func­

necessary for catalysis (99, 100).

Other enzymes in the fungal CoA biosynthesis pathway

(Cab2p–Cab5p) have been  largely understudied. Despite limited biochemical and
