                   TmLOX13 is mainly expressed in the phase of PTX biosynthesis
                          The expression of TmLOX13 was temporally determined during both phases of T. media cell suspension cultures,
                          namely the growth phase (1–12 DAI) and the PTX production phase (12–24 DAI). As shown in Fig. 5A,
                      TmLOX13 transcripts were peaked on day 16 DAI, where a significant increase, about 18-fold, in the TmLOX13
                            gene transcripts was determined comparing with their amount on day 4. The accumulation of TmLOX13 gene
                                 transcripts was synchronized with similar kinetics for the 13-LOX activity (Fig. 5B). In connection with PTX
                                biosynthesis, we found that the increase in the TmLOX13 gene expression occurred at the beginning of PTX
                            production phase, while the maximum production of PTX (4.2 mg L− 1) was detected on day 20 DAI (Fig. 5C).
                               Together, these data indicate that the highest level of TmLOX13 expression in the cell suspension culture of T.
                          media takes place in the beginning of PTX production phase, suggesting a possible biological link between the
                        13-LOX pathway and PTX production.

                   PTX production is drastically affected in the TmLOX13-silenced T. media cells
                          The functional connection of TmLOX13 with the biosynthesis of PTX in T. media cell suspension culture was
                              evaluated by TmLOX13 gene silencing via the siRNA approach. As a result, the TmLOX13 gene transcripts were
                               silenced in the T. media cell suspension as shown by a strong reduction in the level of TmLOX13 transcripts,
                                as well as in the 13-LOX activity compared to wild-type (wt) (Fig. 6A and B). The silencing of TmLOX13 did
                            not significantly affect the growth of T. media cells (Fig. 6C), however, their production of PTX was decreased
