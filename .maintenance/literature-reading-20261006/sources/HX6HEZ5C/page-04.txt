                          Characterization of TmLOX13 enzymatic activity
                          The LOX activity was routinely assayed by continuously monitoring the formation of conjugated dienes at
                           234 nm. Linoleic (C18:2) and linolenic (C18:3) acids were purchased from Sigma-Aldrich. The substrates were
                             prepared according to Gata et al.52,. The standard reaction mixture was composed of 1.5 mL of 100 mM sodium-
                            phosphate buffer pH 6.5, 0.5 mM of fatty acid solution in DMSO and 5 µL (10 µg) purified enzyme at saturating
                           oxygen concentrations, incubated at 25 °C. The increase of hydroperoxidation was detected at 234 nm at 25 °C in
                             a Jenway 6840 spectrophotometer for 3 min and the slope of the linear phase of the plot was used to calculate the
                    LOX activity. Thus, one unit (U) of LOX activity is defined as the amount of enzyme catalyzing the formation of
                           1 µmol of product per minute at 25 °C. The kinetic values of TmLOX13 against linoleic and linolenic acids were
                                calculated using a range of substrate concentrations in standard reaction conditions. The substrate specificity
                          was determined by incubating the recombinant TmLOX13 with 0.5 mM of linoleic (C18:2) and linolenic (C18:3)
                                acids in standard reaction conditions. The optimum pH for the enzymatic activity of TmLOX13 was determined
                             over a pH range of 4.0–9.0 at 25 °C as described by Cao et al.53,. The optimal temperature of the enzymatic
                                   activity was determined over a range of 15–60 °C in increments of 5 °C using the standard assay conditions.
                            Hydroperoxides (-OOH) fatty acids were determined according to Hanano et al.44, using a Jasco LC-2000 plus
                                  series HPLC system (Jasco, USA) with a UV-detector (RF-10Axl, Shimadzu) (234 nm) and a C18 column
                                (Eclipse XDB-C18 150 × 4.6 mm, 5 μm; Agilent Technologies, USA). The analysis was performed using a mobile
                            phase of acetonitrile/water/acetic acid (50/50/0.1, v/v/v) at a flow rate of 0.6 mL min− 1. FA-hydroperoxides were
                               quantified using their respective standards.

                           Transcript analysis
                          The expression profile of TmLOX13 gene during time course of T. media cell suspension cultures were analysed
                           by reverse-transcription quantitative PCR (RT-qPCR). Briefly, samples from whole cells (about 1 g) at stages 4,
                                    8, 12, 16, 20 and 24 days after inoculation (DAI) were taken to extract total RNA using an RNeasy kit according
                                to the manufacturer’s protocol (Qiagen, Germany). An aliquot of 1 µg total RNA was used for first-strand cDNA
                                synthesis according to Hanano et al. (2014)54. Real-time PCR was performed using a AriaMx Real-time PCR
                           System from Agilent technologies, USA. Briefly, 25 µL reaction mixtures contained 0.5 µM of each specific
                               oligonucleotide primer for the target genes (TmLOX13, TXS, DBAT and DBTNBT) and the reference gene (TIP-
                             41) (Table S1), 12.5 µL of SYBR Green PCR mix (Bio-Rad, USA) and 100 ng cDNA. Subsequently, the relative
                                quantification (RQ) of target genes was calculated directly using the software installed in the qPCR system.

                         Extraction and analysis of PTX
                     PTX from the culture media was extracted according to Cusido et al.19,. Briefly, 10 mL of media was mixed and
                             vortexed for 2 min with 5 mL of dichloromethane (DCM), then sonicated for 1 h at 25° C. The lower organic
                            phase of DCM was carefully recovered by a glass Pasteur pipette, replaced in a 5-mL amber vial and evaporated
                                to dryness under a stream of nitrogen gas. The residual PTX was re-suspended into 200 µL acetonitrile. Finally,
                              the extracts were filtered before analysis through a 0.22 μm PVDF filters (Millipore, Billerica, MA, USA). The
                             accuracy of extraction was evaluated using a spiked sample with known concentration of a commercial PTX
                         (T7402-5MG, purity ≥ 95%, Sigma-Aldrich) and the extract recovery (%) was therefore calculated. Quantification
                                of PTX was performed using a Sykam HPLC system (Germany) with a UV-detector at 227 nm. The separation
                                of PTX was performed on a C8 HPLC Column (5 μm particle size, L × I.D. 15 cm × 4.6 mm) (Sigma-Aldrich)
                                as described by Yousfan et al.55,. The following conditions were used: column temp: 25 °C; mobile phase: water:
                                   acetonitrile; (50:50, v/v): flow rate:1 mL min− 1; pressure: 2727 psi (188 bar); injection volume: 5 µL. To avoid any
                                technical variations, an internal standard of PTX (25 µg mL− 1) was added to the experimental samples. The peak
                                areas corresponding to PTX extracted from the samples and its authentic standard were therefore compared.
                          Measurements were performed in triplicate.

                          Silencing of TmLOX13 gene by SiRNA approach
                        siRNA primers used in this study were designed online by siDirect Designer (http://design.RNAi.jp/)56 to target
                              highly conserved region of the mRNA sequence of TmLOX13 gene (accession number NCBI: MW030627).
                          The tool identified 473 potential (21nt)-target regions in the Tm13LOX mRNA that could be degraded by 743
                                  specific siRNA oligos. Among these, only three 21nt target regions were selected, corresponding to specific
                                variant domains in the Tm13LOX protein. These domains were identified through multi-alignment analysis of
                           20 well-characterized LOX isoforms from various plants. The positions of the (21nt)-target regions, siRNA oligo
                              sequences, and additional details are provided in the supplementary information, where the three selected target
                              regions are highlighted in yellow. The silencing efficiency of the three proposed siRNA oligos was tested, and
                                  ultimately, the first oligo, located at position 93–115, was selected. This oligo was designated as siRNATmLOX13
                               (Table S1). Delivery of siRNA to protoplasts was carried out according to57[,58. In a sterile 1.5 ml-centrifuge tube,
                           10 µl of each siRNA was mixed with an equal volume of Lipofectin reagent (Invitrogen Life Technologies, UK)
                         and allowed to stand for 15 min at 20º C. A volume of 50 µl of T. media cells were added and mixed gently. The
                              tubes were incubated at 20º C for 24 h to allow transfection to proceed59. Then the mixture was inoculated in 10
                        ml of growth medium with 1.2 M of sorbitol for 7 days at 28º C in the dark. A similar treatment of cells without
                        siRNA was performed as negative controls. All experiments were carried out using three biological replicates.
