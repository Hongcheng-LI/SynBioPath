Polyketide synthase                             naphthopyrone PKS ATEG_06206 was identified in the genome sequence of A. terreus MEFC01. This gene is silentSilent gene cluster activation                                            under laboratory conditions. To study the function of this PKS, we activated the silent biosynthetic pathway byBiosynthetic pathway                                                  replacing the promoter of cluster-specific transcriptional factor ATEG_06205 in A. terreus MEFC01. With thisNaphthoquinone
                                                      strategy, we confirmed that the products of this cryptic PKS are naphthoquinones. These naphthoquinones are
                                                   soluble pigments, which could be secreted into the agar medium and culture broth. For this reason, the colour of

                                                      cluster and biosynthetic pathway were further elucidated through the reverse genetics approach and enzymatic
                                                 assay in vitro.
